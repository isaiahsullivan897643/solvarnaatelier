Satisfaction Guarantee

Refund & 30-Day Trial

Curated Home Comfort • Processed Exclusively in USD ($)

1. The 30-Day In-Home Trial

We want you to live with our sculpted timber furniture, desk organizers, and lighting under your home's natural ambiance. Every piece comes with an unconditional 30-day in-home trial starting the day of confirmed freight delivery anywhere in the United States.

2. Return Criteria & Item Condition

To qualify for a 100% full refund back to your original payment method:

  • The furniture must be clean, unmarred, and free of stains, chemical odors, pet hair, or custom carpenter drillings.
  • All original solid-brass mounting bolts, felt floor protectors, and wood conditioning wax packets must be returned.
  • Smaller studio accessories (Ceramic Lantern, Desk Organizer) must be repacked into original inner and outer cartons.

3. Freight Return Logistics

For larger furniture (Ergonomic Task Chairs, Dining Tables, Platform Beds), our concierge arranges a scheduled curbside freight pickup directly at your residence. For smaller items, we generate a prepaid courier shipping label. Once received and inspected at our workshop, your refund is issued in 3–5 business days.

4. 10-Year Structural Wood Warranty

Beyond the 30-day trial period, your furniture investment is secured by our 10-Year Craftsmanship Warranty covering timber joinery integrity, wood lamination, and bentwood structural durability against manufacturing failure.
